WALKTHROUGH GUIDE
1. In the beginning you must challenge the only available enemy in Japan to a battle by typing "battle Kakyoin Noriaki".
2. When you have defeated Kakyoin you should be able to see a vehicle appear. Use this vehicle by typing "drive airplane".
3. When you have selected a suitable vehicle you should see all the possible exits. Pick one of the exits with "go" command to move in.
4. In the next location you must again battle a new enemy (if you haven't visited this place before).
5. The same cycle (steps 1-3) repeats with different vehicles and enemies.
6. Once you reach Cairo, the destination of our hero, you must defeat DIO himself (again with "battle" command). Once DIO is defeated you win the game

TIPS
1. Don't move around or do unnecessary stuff too much since there's a time limit (50 turns or days in this context) so you better hurry up.
